# Core Interests

### Healthy Lifestyle & Science

I value an active and healthy lifestyle, with running being one of my preferred activities. It challenges me, reinforces discipline, and motivates continuous improvement.

I am deeply curious about science and enjoy understanding complex subjects. I also enjoy teaching and guiding others, especially when I can make complex ideas clear and accessible.

**Relevant traits:** curiosity, discipline, continuous learning, analytical thinking, communication, mentoring.

### Cooking & Gardening

I enjoy cooking as a way to explore different cultures, experiment with ingredients and techniques, and create something personal. I particularly enjoy preparing healthy meals and exploring ways to make nutritious food.

Growing aromatic herbs has complemented this interest, adding a hands-on dimension to cooking and encouraging attention to detail and experimentation.

**Relevant traits:** creativity, experimentation, cultural curiosity, healthy living, attention to detail, adaptability.

### Video Games & Creative Development

I am driven by creating rather than simply consuming. I enjoy exploring different fields, developing ideas, and turning them into tangible projects.

Video games particularly appeal to me because they combine creativity, technology, and storytelling. I enjoy both playing and creating games, especially designing worlds and characters while exploring areas such as drawing, pixel art, storytelling, and game design.

**Relevant traits:** creativity, problem-solving, systems thinking, storytelling, visual thinking, technical curiosity, project building.
